How to Play

  • Move: Click a piece to select it, then click a highlighted square to move.
  • Capture: Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it. Captures are mandatory.
  • Kings: Reach the far end to become a King (gold crown). Kings can move backwards.
  • Russian variant: Kings "fly" along diagonals and can capture in any direction.
  • Win: Capture all opponent pieces or leave them with no legal moves.
